ℹ️ About This Tool

  • Free & Open Source: No signup, no API keys, runs entirely in your browser
  • Engine: Web Speech API (built into modern browsers)
  • Unlimited Text: Synthesize as much text as you want
  • Voice Support: All voices from your OS + browser
  • Customization: Pitch, rate, volume controls on every voice
  • Languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Mandarin, Hindi, and more
  • Privacy: Text never leaves your computer

🎮 Controls Guide

  • Play: Start speaking the text
  • Pause: Pause mid-speech (resume with Play)
  • Stop: Stop and clear the speech
  • Reverse: Play text in reverse order
  • Forward: Skip ahead in the text
  • Download: Save audio as MP3 (requires backend setup)
  • Pitch: Make voice higher (2x) or lower (0.5x)
  • Speed: Make speech faster (2x) or slower (0.5x)
  • Volume: Control audio loudness (0-100%)
